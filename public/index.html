How to play

Each orange box hides a word; the number is how many letters it has. Lines join related words, and a word sitting between two others connects them both. Find a word and its neighbours appear. The further you get from the middle, the further the words wander from the topic.

Purple words sit on the edge of the board. Finding one opens a whole new board, which gives you another place to start when you're stuck.

Plurals and small spelling slips count. Tap a box, then Hint for a clue, then Letter as many times as you need. Each tap shows one more letter, all the way to the whole word. Drag to move around and pinch to zoom. Boards shows a map of every board you've opened.
